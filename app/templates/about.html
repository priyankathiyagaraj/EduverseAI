What is EduVerse AI?

EduVerse AI is a smart education discovery platform engineered to help students make better, objective decisions about higher education in Tamil Nadu. Choosing the right college and career trajectory is one of the most critical decisions a student makes, yet traditional search engines are crowded with paid advertisements, unverified rankings, and fragmented information.
