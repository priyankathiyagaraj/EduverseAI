Data Quality Model & Academic Integrity

Never Guessing. Always Transparent.

In EduVerse AI, missing information is never treated as zero, and unavailable data is never reported as negative. Every institution profile explicitly displays its source reference, last verification timestamp, and validation status:

VERIFIED

Sourced directly from official university portals or government gazettes.

SAMPLE

Academic development test dataset. Clearly labeled to prevent deception.

ESTIMATED

Calculated from historical public fee notifications or state committee norms.

NOT_AVAILABLE

Explicitly acknowledged as missing rather than coerced to a false number.
